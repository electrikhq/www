Related: [`distribution-channels.md`](distribution-channels.md) Tier C · KP canvas `electrik-keyword-demand`

## Rules

1. **Laravel-intent first** — page must honestly lead to Electrik (Composer package, teams + Stripe on team).  
2. **No doorway spam** — one primary keyword + related cluster; real matrix/steps, not 800 words of fluff.  
3. **Every page:** problem → when Electrik fits / doesn’t → demo + pricing + grant honesty.  
4. **Skip** generic mega-head terms we can’t win soon (`how to make a saas` alone, `multi tenancy` alone, `startup ideas`).  
5. Cap: **1 SEO ship / 2 weeks** (can replace a Wed social ship).
